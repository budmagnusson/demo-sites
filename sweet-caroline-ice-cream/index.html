About

Altona's dessert stop

A small ice cream and dessert shop on 6th Street SE — scoops, baking, and a café-style table to linger at.

Ice cream by day, date-night by night

Sweet Caroline Ice Cream sits at 46 6 St SE in Altona — part ice cream shop, part dessert café. Reviewers have enjoyed waffles with waffle sauce, cheesecake, farmer brownies, chocolate cream pie and puffed wheat cake, and at least one couple brought their own board game and stayed for tea after dessert.

Listings also note coffee, quick bites and small plates, so it's as much a morning coffee stop as an evening ice cream run. It opens early — 6 AM most days.

Good to know: hours vary across the different online listings, and there's no official website — a quick call before heading out is the safest bet.
