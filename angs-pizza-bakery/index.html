About

Altona's pizza & bakery spot

A hometown restaurant on 2nd Avenue NE serving pizza, burgers, chicken — and baked goods from the bakery side.

More than just pizza

Ang's Pizza & Bakery sits at 58 2nd Avenue NE in Altona. Regulars describe thin, crispy pizzas loaded with toppings, hearty burgers, and portions that keep people coming back — all with dine-in, takeout and delivery.

The bakery side is the surprise: cinnamon buns, cheesecake and — in season — soft-serve ice cream. One stop covers the meal and the dessert.

Good to know: you may also see this business listed as "Ang's Pizza & More" — same Altona restaurant, same phone number. A few listings show "2 Ave NW", but the Google listing and the local street grid put it on 2nd Ave NE.
