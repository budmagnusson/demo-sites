Our Story

A potpourri, in the truest sense

A cozy Altona kitchen blending Filipino classics with Canadian comfort food.

Two food cultures, one table

Potpourri of Flavours brings the bold, savoury-sweet flavours of the Philippines together with the hearty dishes Canadians love. Think garlic-kissed chicken next to a juicy burger, spring rolls beside poutine — every plate a little mix of both worlds.

It's a warm, family-friendly spot with friendly staff, a sunny patio in summer, and food that keeps locals coming back — some driving in from over an hour away just for lunch.

Catering? Listed as a caterer too — call and ask about feeding your next family gathering or celebration.

Their sunny patio — "Patio de Potpourri", serving Altona since 2019.
