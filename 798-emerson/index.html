About 798

A Hidden Gem at the Border

798 Emerson is a Chinese restaurant on Dallas Avenue serving up Chinese food, sushi and ramen — from spicy California rolls and Dynamite Crunch to sashimi boxes and a weekend lunch buffet. Friendly owners, generous portions, and flavours that keep locals and travellers coming back.
