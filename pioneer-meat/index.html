OUR STORY

Fifty years. One recipe. Zero shortcuts.

Farmers sausage might seem like a strange thing to be passionate about — but it's our passion.

Pioneer Meat has been a tradition in Altona, Manitoba for over 50 years. We've painstakingly developed and guarded our recipe and process — and even as the business has grown, what hasn't changed are the recipes and the principles behind them. Everything we do is dedicated to one thing: the best possible experience for our customers.

WHAT WE STILL BELIEVE

  • We still use minimal ingredients
  • We still sell only the freshest products
  • We buy and produce everything locally
  • We are guided by simple, strong values
  • All our products are gluten free
