Our Story

From Korea to Emerson,
with love (and bulgogi)

The Depot is run by Minho and Eunhee, a Korean couple who moved to Emerson in 2022 with their two kids — and fell in love with rural Manitoba life.

When they took over the clubhouse, they brought their kitchen with them: the comfort food of two cultures, side by side. Their grand opening in April 2025 kicked off a new chapter for the nine-hole course's gathering place.

Today The Depot is a year-round public restaurant where golfers, neighbours and road-trippers share tables — and where a small-town clubhouse smells faintly of garlic, sesame and fresh-cut grass.

"We came for a quieter life. We stayed because the people here made us feel at home — so we cook for them like family."

— The spirit of Minho & Eunhee's kitchen at The Depot
A Monthly Tradition

Korean Special Nights

Once a month, The Depot goes full Korean. Minho and Eunhee cook the dishes they grew up with — the kind of food that fills a table and keeps everyone talking long after the plates are empty.

Bulgogi

Sweet-savoury marinated beef, grilled hot

Samgyeopsal

Grilled pork belly, wraps & all the fixings

Bibimbap

Colourful rice bowls, mixed at the table

Spicy Pork Rice Bowls

Bold, fiery and deeply comforting

Korean Special Nights run monthly — dates are announced on The Depot's Facebook page. These evenings are popular, so calling ahead is a good idea.
The Kitchen

Comfort food, both cultures

The everyday menu is pure Manitoba clubhouse: hearty, handmade and generous. A few house favourites below — ask in-house for the full menu.

Mennonite Poutine

A local twist on the classic — a nod to the Mennonite kitchens of the Pembina Valley, done the Depot way.

Handmade Burgers

Built from scratch in-house — the kind of burger that needs two hands and a stack of napkins.

Pizza

Clubhouse pizza done right — a crowd-pleaser for the whole table after nine holes.

Fully licensed. Enjoy a cold one with your meal — the bar is stocked and the deck chairs are waiting.

The Course

Nine holes of prairie golf

The Depot sits right at the nine-hole Emerson Golf Course — a friendly, small-town track with a story of resilience behind it.

  • 9

    Nine holes — perfect for a relaxed round, a league night or learning the game.

  • 2016

    Rebuilt in 2016 after flood years took their toll — the course came back stronger.

  • 365

    Open year-round. The restaurant keeps serving through every season — golf weather or not, there's always a table waiting.
