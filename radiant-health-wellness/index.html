Wellness, close to home

Radiant Health & Wellness is a wellness clinic on Seventh Street in Gretna, Manitoba, aiming to help clients live their healthiest life. The clinic is best known for specializing in allergy treatments — a specialty named in its own public business description.

Good to know: the clinic is closed on weekends and stays open late — until 7 PM — on Thursdays. Call (204) 712-6315 to book; there is currently no online booking or website.
