About

A small-town ice cream hut

Altona's stop for soft serve and diner-style burgers — open for the season, when Manitoba can handle ice cream.

Seasonal ice cream and burgers

The Ice Cream Hut describes itself as a seasonal ice cream and burger restaurant at 63 4 Ave NE in Altona. It's a summer-time spot: cone weather is when the hut is open.

The Hut Burger — served with fries and gravy — is the dish regulars mention most, and reviews single out the soft-serve ice cream as one of the best in southern Manitoba.

Good to know: there is no published menu online, and listing sites disagree on closing times (8–10 PM). Check the hours below and call ahead to confirm what's open today.
