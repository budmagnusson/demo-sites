About

A cozy kitchen in the Altona Mall

The family restaurant where good food and good people meet — right in the middle of Altona Mall.

Homestyle, all day

Oma's Kitchen is the kind of place that feels like Sunday dinner — a warm, family-run restaurant inside the Altona Mall serving breakfast, lunch and dinner with a Mennonite heart.

"Where good food & good people meet" — the restaurant's own motto, and 4.4 stars across 85 Google reviews says customers agree.

Good to know: Oma's is closed on Sundays, and opens an hour early — 7 AM — on Fridays and Saturdays. Check the hours below before you head out.
